How QR Codes Work

1. It’s just printed text A QR code doesn’t store a live file or connect to a server on its own — it simply turns text (like a web address, WiFi password, or contact card) into a scannable pattern.
2. Changing a link later Because the pattern is permanent once printed, use a URL forwarder if the destination might change — ideally a redirect on a domain you own.
3. Scanning tips Keep strong contrast (dark dots on a light background), leave a quiet margin around the edges, and always test-scan with your phone before printing.
